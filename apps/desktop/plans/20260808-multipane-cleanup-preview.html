1. The overlap, and why it happens

The account strip is position:absolute; inset-x-0; justify-center across the whole header (SessionView.tsx:129). It is not in the layout flow, so it does not push anything aside and nothing reserves room for it. At full width it looks fine. Narrow the pane and it slides straight under the folder name on the left and the context number on the right. This is the bug, and it is there at any pane width once the header runs out of room.
