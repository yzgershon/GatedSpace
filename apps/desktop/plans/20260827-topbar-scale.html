What changes, and why

  • The band, not the bar. Under a floating shell there is no line between the bar and the trough beneath it — both paint the same well colour — so the space above the cards is 48px of bar plus the 18px gutter. Its midline is 33px. Centring in the bar's own box was measurably centred and visibly high, which is exactly how it was reported.
  • One full gutter of padding-top on the bar moves its content box to 18..48, whose centre is 33. The bar's OUTER height is untouched, so the cards below do not move.
  • Derived from --gs-pane-inset, not gated on the skin. VS Code Style sets paneGap: 0, so the padding evaluates to 0 and that skin's toolbar keeps centring in its own box — right for a bar that has a fill, a rule under it, and no trough at all.
  • A taller band affords a taller row. 46px surfaces with 40px controls inside, 15px labels and 20px glyphs, leaving 10px clear at each end of the band.
  • The wordmark goes to 18px and stays the largest thing in the bar. It is a name, not a control; matching it to the buttons removes the only hierarchy the row has.
