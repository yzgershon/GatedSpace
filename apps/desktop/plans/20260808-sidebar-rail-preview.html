The left rail — what's off, and five ways out

Every colour below is copied from globals.css. Each panel is the real structure: a 48px rail, three centred icons, Settings and Help pinned at the bottom, against a mock sessions panel.

The problem isn't the icons. The rail and the panel are both bg-sidebar — the same #1a1716. The only thing between them is border-r border-border/45, which is #2a2827 at 45% over #1a1716: about a 4% step, invisible in practice. So there is no toolbar, just three dim glyphs floating in the sessions panel's margin. Centring them made it read stronger, because now nothing anchors them top or bottom.

What I'd pick, and why

A or B. Both fix the actual fault — the rail becomes a surface instead of empty margin — and neither adds a new colour to the app. B is the more conventional answer and reads best when the panel is closed, since a filled tile survives without a neighbouring edge to sit against.

C and D are louder than this element earns. The rail is navigation you use a few times an hour; the accent is spent on agent identity elsewhere in the app, and spending it here too dilutes that. D in particular fights the pane ring, which is the one thing in the window that is supposed to glow.

E is the one to take if the emptiness is what bothers you rather than the boundary. It gives the centred group a container, so it stops looking like three icons adrift. It can be combined with A or B.

On A + D, now that it is built: it works, and the middle strength is the one. The recessed rail is doing the heavy lifting — that is the fix — and the glow is decoration riding on top of a boundary that now exists. Two things to keep in mind. It still spends the accent on navigation, so if a Claude pane is focused you will have the same orange glowing in two places at once. And a glow cannot be the only thing carrying selection, which is why the edge bar stayed. Take the subtle one if the double-orange bothers you once it is running; it is a one-number change either way.
