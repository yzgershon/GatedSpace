This is lucide's ArrowDownToLine, copied path-for-path out of the package, so it is literally the icon the app renders — not a sketch of one. A download-to-line arrow rather than a refresh spiral: refresh means reload, this installs a build. It also leaves its top-right corner empty, which is where the dot sits; a circular icon runs its own stroke under the badge and the two smear together at this size.

Green dot with a slow ping, same green as "agent done" — in this app green already means something finished, go look, and a finished build is exactly that. Hovering says which version. Clicking warns first if a session is mid-turn, then quits, installs and relaunches.
