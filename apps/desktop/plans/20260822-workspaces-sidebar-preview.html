Press After and watch the left column only. Three group headers and a 44px indent go; the session rows come out one level and say what each agent is actually doing. The rail on the right is unchanged, because it is now the thing that answers "which groups exist".

Why the group level can go

It was added for a specific reason, and the reason expired. From DashboardSidebarWorkspaceSessions.tsx, its own doc comment:

"The group level matters more now than it did, because the tab strip is gone under this skin" — the sidebar became the only place you could see the shape of what was open.

The rail brings that back, and better than the strip did: every group, its status, its pane count, in the title bar. Keeping the same information nested in the sidebar is saying it twice and paying a level of indentation for it.

The duplication is exact, not approximate. A pane only registers in session-activity once it has MOUNTED, so the sidebar's session tree only ever fills in for the workspace you are already in. That is the same one workspace the rail is describing. Every other workspace shows nothing under it either way.

What A costs, and how to pay it

The failure mode to design against. Flattening loses the one thing the nesting was good at: which three sessions share a screen. With six sessions under a workspace you can no longer tell that three of them are one group.

That answer belongs on the rail, not the sidebar — and it is already half built. The strip had a hover card listing each pane in a tab, and getTabIcon was written for those rows across all eight pane kinds. It has been unreachable since the strip was deleted. Hovering a rail tab should list its panes, and then the question is answered where the groups actually live.

One thing to weigh before I build it

Session rows say the LAST PROMPT, and prompts are long. At 280px with a 30px indent there are about 30 characters before the truncation. "audit the restyle batch against the packaged asar" becomes "audit the restyle batch aga…", which is enough to tell two sessions apart and not enough to read.

Flattening buys back 14px of that. If you find yourself widening the sidebar to read them, the answer is not a wider sidebar — it is going back to the derived title on the row and putting the prompt in the tooltip. Say so and I will switch it.

Order I would build it

  1. Flatten the sidebar. One component, no store changes, reversible in a line. This is the half you can judge by looking.
  2. Pane rows on the rail hover. Restores what flattening removes, and brings getTabIcon back from the dead.
