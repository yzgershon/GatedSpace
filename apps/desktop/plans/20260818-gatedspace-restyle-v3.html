What changed from the reference, and why

Everything structural is matched. Three things are deliberately different, and each is because your app is not his:

  • No Agent | Code | Chat switcher. His Code tab is your entire app; you would ship two empty tabs. That prime centre slot goes to the preset launcher instead — which is also how you get the centred, larger presets you asked for.
  • Proportional text, not monospace. His panes are terminals so everything is mono. Yours fold stream-json into a transcript — prose is proportional, tool arguments and output stay mono. Forcing mono everywhere would undo the session view.
  • Windows controls, not traffic lights. The rounded, floating look you liked comes from the card gutters and radius, not from macOS chrome.
One thing to weigh before I build it. Merging the presets into the header removes a whole row, which is most of why this reads cleaner than what you have now. But it caps how many presets fit — five is comfortable at this width, and beyond that they would need to scroll or collapse. You currently show every pinned preset. If you routinely pin more than five, say so and I will keep the separate bar and just centre it.
