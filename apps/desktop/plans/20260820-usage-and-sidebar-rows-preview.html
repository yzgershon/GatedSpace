My pick is D. The bar being a 2px hairline UNDER the name rather than a chunky block beside it is what makes it read as an app rather than a dashboard widget — and it is the only one of the four where the account, the number, the bar and the reset all fit without the row getting taller than the account row above it. A is the runner-up and is the most "abstract" of them. C looks good in isolation but a 26px dial next to a 19px avatar in the row below fights it.

Three things changed in the child rows, which is the part you flagged. They show the last prompt sent to that pane rather than a derived title. The dot is green by default, amber while a turn is running, red on an error or an exhausted limit — it was grey for "nothing to report", which is why every row in your screenshot looked dead. And the elapsed time on the right gives the column something to line up against, which is what the workspace rows already had and the child rows did not.

The workspace name badge. Those two-letter tiles replace the round photo avatars. The photos were the loudest thing in the sidebar and the least informative — every row had a different hue fighting the status dots below it. A monochrome initial tile is the same shape as the row's other slots and lets the dots be the only colour in the panel.
