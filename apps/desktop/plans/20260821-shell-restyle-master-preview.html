Tidy — you are right, and the slot has a real occupant

I am dropping the layout picker. Checking it against the code rather than defending it: panes are already arranged by buildBalancedTree when they are created, so two panes are side by side and four are a 2×2 without anyone asking. A picker whose options are mostly what already happened is a button you press once, see nothing change, and never press again — which is exactly the complaint you have about Tidy today. Same button, new label.

But removing the tab strip orphans something real. The chip reading "1 shell running in the background" in your screenshot is BackgroundTerminalsButton, and the tab strip is its only mount point — it is rendered through renderTabBarTrailing and nowhere else. Delete the strip and it silently disappears, taking with it the only signal that a shell is still alive somewhere you cannot see.

Why the borders can go

The thin lines are each pane's own 1px border, plus the sidebar card's right edge. They were added when the cards had no surface of their own and needed something to describe their shape. Now that the card is lighter than the trough it floats on, the border is a second boundary drawn on top of one that already reads — which is exactly the "thin gray lines" you are seeing.

#0d0a09 the trough #1c1918 the card #2a2827 the border being removed

The card sits 11 values above the trough. The border sits 13 above the card. Removing it loses nothing and removes every line you pointed at, including the one down the right of the sidebar.
